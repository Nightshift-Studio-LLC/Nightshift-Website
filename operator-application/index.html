Nightshift LLC / Community Operations

Server Operator
Application

Nightshift is preparing to bring on reliable operators who can help keep our community servers and player-facing services running smoothly while the studio focuses on building. Assignments may support one or more Nightshift projects as needs develop.

Draft application - role details, hours, and availability may change before intake opens.

The assignment

Keep the service stable.

Operators are a calm, reliable first response for the player community. You do not need to be a developer, but you should be technically curious, able to follow a process, and clear about when something needs escalation.

What you will handle

  • Monitor player reports and basic server health during agreed coverage windows.
  • Apply community rules consistently and document incidents with useful context.
  • Capture reproducible bug reports, evidence, and player impact for development teams.
  • Help coordinate scheduled restarts, updates, and player-facing status communication.

What we are looking for

  • Clear written communication and sound judgment with players.
  • Reliable availability you can state honestly; regular coverage matters more than constant coverage.
  • Server operations, moderation, support, or multiplayer-community experience is helpful but not mandatory.
  • Respect for private player information, internal tooling, and escalation boundaries.

How selection works

Simple intake. Clear expectations.

  1. Submit: Tell us how you would contribute and when you are usually available.
  2. Review: We review for judgment, communication, and fit - not just playtime.
  3. Conversation: Shortlisted applicants may be invited to a brief follow-up.
  4. Trial coverage: Selected operators begin with a scoped, documented trial period.
