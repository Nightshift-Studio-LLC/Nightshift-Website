Nightshift / Arma Homefront

Server Operator
Application

Arma Homefront will need steady hands behind the server as the community opens up. This is a practical operations role: helping players, keeping an eye on the live environment, and escalating issues clearly when they need development attention.

Draft application - details and availability may change before intake opens.

The assignment

Keep the front stable.

Operators are a reliable first response for the player community. You do not need to be a developer, but you should be calm under pressure, technically curious, and comfortable following an established process.

What you will handle

  • Monitor player reports and basic server health during agreed coverage windows.
  • Apply community rules consistently and document incidents with useful context.
  • Capture reproducible bug reports, evidence, and player impact for the development team.
  • Help coordinate restarts, updates, and player-facing status communication when scheduled.

What we are looking for

  • Clear written communication and sound judgment with players.
  • Reliable availability you can state honestly; regular coverage matters more than constant coverage.
  • Experience with Arma Reforger, community servers, moderation, or support is helpful but not mandatory.
  • Respect for private player information, internal tooling, and escalation boundaries.

How selection works

Simple intake. Clear expectations.

  1. Submit: Tell us how you would contribute and when you are usually available.
  2. Review: We review for judgment, communication, and fit - not just playtime.
  3. Conversation: Shortlisted applicants may be invited to a brief follow-up.
  4. Trial coverage: Selected operators begin with a scoped, documented trial period.
